02 The Studio

Every Celebration
Begins With an Idea.

Atithya began with a simple observation — the families who enjoyed their own celebrations most were the ones who weren't managing them.

So we built a studio that takes the whole weight of an event: the concept, the drawings, the vendor contracts, the run sheet and the thousand small decisions nobody warns you about. What we hand back is the part that actually matters — a day you get to be present for.

We take on a limited number of events each season. That's deliberate. It means your florist is briefed by the same person who drew your floor plan, and that on the day itself, someone who knows your family by name is standing quietly at the door.

04 Services

What we take
off your hands.

Engage us for the whole event, or for the single piece that's keeping you awake.

  1. 01

    Event Concept & Planning

    We start with the guest list, the budget and the feeling you're after — then build the concept, the timeline and the cost sheet that everything else is measured against.

  2. 02

    Décor & Styling

    Palette, florals, linen, lighting, signage and tabletop — drawn to scale and sampled in advance, so what arrives on the day is what you approved.

  3. 03

    Venue Setup

    Floor plans, guest flow, stage and seating, power and access. We walk the venue early and solve the awkward corners before they become the day's problem.

  4. 04

    Vendor Coordination

    Catering, photography, sound, entertainment, transport. We brief them, negotiate them, chase them and keep a single schedule that everyone works from.

  5. 05

    Event-Day Management

    A dedicated coordinator and crew on site from load-in to load-out, holding the run sheet so no one in your family has to answer a single vendor call.

  6. 06

    Custom Experiences

    The details people remember — a live counter, a surprise entry, a hand-drawn menu, a ritual reimagined for a room your grandmother would still recognise.

Typical lead time 10–14 weeks

05 The Experience

From the first idea to
the final celebration.

One team, one thread, five stages. You'll never be asked to explain your event twice.

  1. Concept

    We meet, we listen, and we come back with a direction — a palette, a mood and a shape for the day.

  2. Planning

    Budget, venue, guest count and calendar are locked. You get one document that holds every decision.

  3. Design

    Layouts drawn to scale, florals sampled, linen and lighting chosen. Nothing is left to be imagined.

  4. Coordination

    Vendors briefed and contracted, deliveries sequenced, a run sheet circulated to everyone involved.

  5. Execution

    Our crew arrives before anyone else and leaves after everyone else. You simply attend your own celebration.
